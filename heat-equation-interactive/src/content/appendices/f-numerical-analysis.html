Appendix F · Numerical analysis and reproducibility

When to use this appendix. Use this appendix to interpret numerical error, mesh refinement, and reproducibility; an empirical rate is not an unconditional convergence theorem.

Use the weighted grid inner product of Proposition 11.4. Each interior edge contributes \(-\ell\omega_k (u_i-u_j)(v_i-v_j)\) in horizontal directions and \(-\ell(u_i-u_j)(v_i-v_j)\) vertically. This follows by adding the two endpoint terms; the half-weight at the bottom makes the doubled coefficient symmetric. With zero Dirichlet data this summation-by-parts formula extends to arbitrary grid vectors, and the Robin term adds \(\ell^2\sum V u_0v_0\). Setting \(v=u\) gives the discrete energy pairing, and taking \(v=1\) on active nodes gives the outer-face loss.

For an exact solution with bounded time derivatives through order two and spatial derivatives through order four on the closed active region, Taylor's formula with bounded remainders establishes the residual bounds used in Proposition 11.3. They are assumptions, not consequences of numerical stability. If \(E(h)\approx Ch^p\) with comparable time error, the ratio \(\log_2(E(h)/E(h/2))\) estimates the order; self-convergence substitutes inter-grid differences and is not a certified error estimate. The fixed-time-step spatial test can eventually be limited by temporal error.
